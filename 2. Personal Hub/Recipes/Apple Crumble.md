## Ingredients (8)

- 150g (1 cup) plain flour
    
- 100g (1/2 cup, firmly packed) brown sugar
    
- 50g (1/2 cup) Coles rolled oats
    
- 100g chilled butter, chopped
    
- 60g (1/2 cup) chopped walnuts
    
- 1 x 800g can pie [apple](https://www.taste.com.au/recipes/collections/apple-recipes)
    
- Icing sugar, for dusting
    
- Vanilla ice-cream, to serve


## Method

Show ingredient quantity

- Step 1
    
    Preheat oven to 180C. Combine the 150g (1 cup) plain flour, 100g (1/2 cup, firmly packed) brown sugar, 50g (1/2 cup) rolled oats and 100g chilled butter, chopped in a bowl.
    
- Step 2
    
    Use your fingertips to rub the butter into the flour mixture until the mixture resembles fine breadcrumbs. Stir in the 60g (1/2 cup) chopped walnuts.
    
- Step 3
    
    Spoon the 1 x 800g can pie apple into a 1.5L (6-cup) capacity ovenproof dish. Scatter the walnut mixture evenly over the apples. Bake in oven for 20-25 minutes or until golden. Dust with Icing sugar, for dusting. Spoon the apple crumble into serving bowls. Serve with Vanilla ice-cream, to serve.
