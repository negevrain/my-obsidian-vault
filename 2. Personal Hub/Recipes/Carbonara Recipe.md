- 3 eggs + 1 yolk
- 100ml thickened cream
- 2/3 cup Parmesan cheese
- Salt and pepper 




1 tbs olive oil in a frypan

Add 300g of chopped bacon

Add 1/2 cup reserved pasta water

Add egg mixture

Have extra parsley and Parmesan for serving.
