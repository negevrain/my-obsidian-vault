Ingredients:
	•	1 pound ground beef
	•	1 tablespoon olive oil
	•	1/2 cup chopped onion
	•	1 clove garlic, minced
	•	1/2 teaspoon salt
	•	1/4 teaspoon black pepper
	•	1/2 cup broccoli florets
	•	1/2 cup chicken broth
	•	1/4 cup heavy cream
	•	1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Instructions:
	1.	Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the ground beef and cook, breaking it up with a spatula, until browned.
	2.	Add the onion and garlic and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
	3.	Season with salt and pepper.
	4.	Add the broccoli and chicken broth and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to low and simmer for 5 minutes, or until the broccoli is tender.
	5.	Stir in the heavy cream and Parmesan cheese. Serve over pasta.
