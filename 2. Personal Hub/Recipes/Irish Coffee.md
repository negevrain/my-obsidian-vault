## Ingredients 
•	2 tbsp double cream
•	150ml freshly brewed black coffee
•	50ml Irish whiskey
•	½ - 1 tsp brown sugar
•	pinch freshly grated nutmeg


## Instructions
•	STEP 1 - Lightly whip the cream just so it’s very slightly thickened, then set aside.   
•	STEP 2 - Pour the hot coffee into a mug or heatproof glass, then add the whiskey and sugar. Stir until the sugar has dissolved.
•	STEP 3 - Gently float the cream on the top and sprinkle the nutmeg over the cream.

Serve hot.
