## Ingredients
- 500g lean beef mince
- 1 large egg (for binding and extra protein)
- 1 small onion, finely grated (or finely diced zucchini if you want to amp up the veggies without carbs)
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp dried herbs (like oregano or thyme)
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp almond flour (optional, for a touch of binding; omit for strict no-carb)
- 2 tbsp olive oil (for frying)
- Fresh parsley or chives, chopped (for garnish, optional)

## Instructions
1. In a big bowl, combine the beef mince, egg, grated onion (or zucchini), garlic, herbs, salt, pepper, and almond flour if using. Get your hands in there—mix it all until it’s evenly blended but don’t overwork it, or they’ll toughen up.
2. Shape the mixture into 8 flat patties, about 1cm thick. If they’re sticky, wet your hands a bit. Pop them on a plate and chill in the fridge for 15-20 minutes to firm up.
3. Heat the olive oil in a non-stick fry pan over medium heat. Cook the rissoles for 4-5 minutes per side, until they’re golden-brown outside and cooked through (internal temp around 70°C if you’re precise like that).
4. Drain on paper towel, garnish with herbs if you fancy, and serve hot. Pair with a side salad or steamed greens—no potatoes or bread in sight.

## Notes
Per two rissoles: Roughly 300 calories, 25g protein, 0g carbs (if no almond flour), 20g fat. Total batch is under 5g net carbs. They’ll store in the fridge for 3 days or freeze beautifully.
