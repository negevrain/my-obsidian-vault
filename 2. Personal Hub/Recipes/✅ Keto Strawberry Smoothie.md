# Ingredients (serves 1)

- 150g frozen strawberries (about 6–8 berries, ~4g net carbs; they add sweetness and a creamy texture without much carb load)
- 30g unsweetened whey protein powder (vanilla or unflavoured, ~1g carbs, ~25g protein; check for no added sugars)
- 200ml unsweetened almond milk (0.5–1g carbs; keeps it creamy but low-carb)
- 100ml thickened cream (since you mentioned it’s very low carb, ~3g carbs, adds richness and healthy fats)
- 1 tsp chia seeds (optional, ~0.5g net carbs, adds fibre and thickness)
- 1–2 tsp erythritol or stevia (to taste, 0 carbs; natural, keto-friendly sweeteners)
- Ice cubes (4–5, optional for extra chill and texture)

Total estimated macros (approximate, varies by brand):
•  Carbs: ~5–6g net carbs
•  Protein: ~25–28g
•  Fat: ~30g (from cream and almond milk)
•  Calories: ~350–400 kcal

Instructions:
1. Toss the frozen strawberries, protein powder, almond milk, and cream into a blender.
2. Add chia seeds and sweetener if using, plus ice cubes for a frosty vibe.
3. Blend on high for 30–45 seconds until smooth and creamy. If it’s too thick, add a splash more almond milk (10–20ml).
4. Pour into a chilled glass and enjoy immediately to keep that vibrant strawberry zing.
