
**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound ground beef or ground turkey
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 1 bell pepper, diced (any colour you prefer)
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 teaspoons Worcestershire sauce
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1 teaspoon dried rosemary
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cups beef or vegetable broth
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- Mashed potatoes or cooked rice for serving (optional)

**Instructions:**

1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.

2. Add the chopped onion, garlic, and diced carrots to the skillet. Sauté for about 5 minutes or until the vegetables begin to soften.

3. Add the ground beef or turkey to the skillet and cook until it's browned and crumbled. Use a spatula to break it into small pieces as it cooks.

4. Sprinkle the flour over the meat and stir well to combine. This will help thicken the sauce.

5. Add the Worcestershire sauce, dried thyme, dried rosemary, salt, and pepper to the skillet. Stir to evenly distribute the seasonings.

6. Pour in the beef or vegetable broth, stirring constantly. Bring the mixture to a simmer, and then reduce the heat to low. Let it simmer for about 10-15 minutes, or until the mixture thickens and the vegetables are tender.

7. Add the diced bell pepper and frozen peas to the skillet. Continue to simmer for an additional 5-7 minutes, or until the peas are heated through and the bell pepper is slightly softened. Adjust the seasoning with more salt and pepper if needed.

8. Serve the savory mince over mashed potatoes or cooked rice if desired, and enjoy!

This savory mince is a hearty and flavorful dish that doesn't rely on tomatoes for its taste. You can customize it further by adding your favorite herbs and spices to suit your preferences.
