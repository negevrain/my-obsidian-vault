If God wanted to, then He could heal my Dad in a heartbeat... completely reverse the dementia, and give my Dad more time to be my Dad.

But if God doesn’t want to, then why does He allow my Dad to go through what he’s going through... to live a life where he can’t control what his body does, where he no longer knows the people who love him, a life marked by indignity, humiliation... the degradation of a man that has served God faithfully all of his adult life.

Am I being selfish for asking these questions?

Perhaps...

I know that God can do whatever He chooses to do. Still, I find it hard to reconcile a father, a “loving” God, allowing one of his kids to go through what my Dad is going through. There’s doesn’t even seem to be a learning opportunity for my Dad ... HE HAS DEMENTIA.

And if this is meant to be some learning / growth opportunity for those of us who love my Dad... well, that’s just sick...

So...
