## 1. Story-Led Appeals

_Testimonies build trust. Capture → Curate → Amplify._

### Testimony Bank DB

`/table` → Inline

**Properties**:

- Name (Title)

- Type (Select: Adult / Child / Pastor / Volunteer)

- Format (Multi-select: Video / Audio / Written)

- Impact Quote (Text)

- Consent Given? (Checkbox)

## 2. Donor Touchpoints

_Every interaction asks, thanks, and invites deeper._

### Digital Donate Flow

- **Landing Page**: notion.site/[appeal] or Carrd

- **Must-Haves**:  
    Apple Pay / Google Pay  
    SMS Giving ($10 text-to-donate)  
    Mobile Field + **Pre-ticked opt-in** (“Keep me in the story”)  
    Progress Bar (embed via Progressier)

### Physical Swag

- **QR Stickers**: Link to micro-donate + survey

- **Issue With**: Every mailout, receipt, church bulletin

> Synced Block → Paste into proposals, emails, social:
> 
> _“Scan to give $10 now — and hear how your gift changes lives.”_
