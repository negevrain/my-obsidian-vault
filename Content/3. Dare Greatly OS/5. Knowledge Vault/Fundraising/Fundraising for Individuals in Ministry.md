## Foundations

Budget and Goals: Create a clear, transparent annual budget breaking down personal living expenses (salary, housing, insurance, etc.) and ministry costs. Set specific monthly and annual support targets. Share this openly with potential supporters to build trust and show accountability.

Giving Page: Set up a dedicated personal ministry website or landing page (e.g., via Donorbox, Tithe.ly, Pushpay, or a simple WordPress site) as the central hub. Include bio, vision, impact stories, budget summary, prayer requests, and secure online donation options (recurring/one-time, tax-deductible if applicable).

Prayer Partnership: Frame support as partnership in ministry—invite prayer first, then financial giving. Regularly share specific prayer updates alongside impact stories.

## Relationships

Personal Network: Cautiously curate and expand—start with family/friends/church, then referrals, church presentations, and contacts within ministry precinct.

Solicited Contacts: Make clear, vision-led asks in presentations, emails, and meetings (e.g., "Would you pray about partnering monthly at $50?").

Corporate Contacts: Within precinct of ministry activities.

Initial Expansion: Schedule church/ministry presentations; ask existing supporters for referrals; attend events/networks related to your field.

## Platforms & Channels

Email; text for those who opt in; monthly online meet ups (optional and dependent upon willingness of ministry leader to engage); face-to-face meet ups; social media; personal website/giving page.

## Message

Impact stories; prayer updates; requests for financial support with link to giving page; information/education (why the ministry matters, budget transparency); personal field updates.

Direct Asks: In communications and meet-ups, clearly invite partnership (financial and prayer) tied to vision, not just need.

## Frequency

Monthly emails (with ad hoc comms) - aim for Tuesday or Wednesday during first week of each month; daily/weekly social media posts with link to donation page.

## Strategy

Supporter List: Build comprehensive list of prospective and actual supporters. Use CRM (Agile CRM recommended) to organise list, segment (e.g., prospects, one-time, recurring, major), track interactions, and personalise comms.

First Email: Invite people to keep receiving updates from ministry leader. Invite to first online meet up. Include vision overview and link to giving page.

Online Meet Ups: Keep duration to 30 minutes max. Share vision, stories, prayer needs; make a soft ask. Send recording to people on mailing list.

SMS: Invite to next online meet up - include invite/link to join; reminder just before; thank-yous post-gift.

Donor Stewardship and Gratitude: Send prompt, personalised thank-yous (email then handwritten note/phone call) for every gift. Provide annual tax receipts. Periodically update on specific impact of their giving. Invite existing supporters to increase or refer others.

Metrics and Review: Track email open/giving rates, donation trends, retention via CRM. Review quarterly—what's working, adjust accordingly.

Compliance: Use secure platform for tax-deductible giving if possible (e.g., partner with sending org). Ensure privacy and transparency.
