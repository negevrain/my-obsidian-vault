Dear Phil,

Every now and then, we hear a story that reminds us just how important this ministry is to the people of Darwin.

The brother of a close friend shared his story. His family is struggling to make ends meet. He runs his own business but it’s tanking because he’s having to be both dad and mum to his kids while his wife deals with severe mental and physical health issues. It’s been difficult for them to do church like a normal family, but… he finds love and hope in his darkness almost everyday when he listens to his local Christian radio station.

Now, this listener doesn’t live in Darwin, but his story is not that uncommon. While most of us enjoy more good days than bad, there are people all over Darwin and the top end that are experiencing their own forms of darkness. For some, the darkness results from losing a job, or worse, losing a loved one. Other people find that their dreams are crumbling to ashes, or they wonder if their kids will ever return home. Still more struggle to breathe in loveless, even abusive, relationships.
