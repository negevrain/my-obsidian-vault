# The DNA of Dare Greatly

DARE GREATLY is a transformation agency that was founded in 2015. We work with pastors, politicians and CEOs as they transform their leadership, and we work with churches, NFP organisations and businesses as they transform the lives of those they serve.

We work exclusively with organisations that deliver 100% of their services in Australia - organisations that aspire to be well run, and meet the needs of the poor, the marginalised, the lost and disconnected. We help provide direct funding, resources and other support as well as development.

### My WHY...
My "why" is predicated on 3 beliefs and 1 driving ambition:
- **Belief 1: Leadership starts in the home, not in the office or the pulpit.** I experienced burn out too many times, and it almost cost me my marriage and my family. I certainly lost friends and valued colleagues as a result. I know I was never fully able to operate at the same level after my last experience. If I have any say in the matter, I want to help other Christian leaders avoid the experiences I had because of pride, arrogance, an unwillingness to have a coach or supervisor walk with me.
- **Belief 2: Christian leadership should be the model for leadership in general.** I cringe at what passes for Christian leadership today. I believe Christian leaders should be setting the standard that the secular world follows, especially since we have the greatest leadership role model in Jesus.
- **Belief 3: God can use our mediocrity, but He's not happy about it.** Too many churches, Christian ministries and Christian NFP organisations (and their respective leaders) seem to settle for mediocrity, and I do not believe that is okay. See Malachi 1:6-14; Colossians 3:23; Matthew 25:14-30.
- **Ambition: I want to build a legacy of transformation that continues after I'm gone, and I would LOVE it if my children took over from me.**

Our vision is to catalyse a movement of Christian leaders who lead like Jesus, pursue excellence over mediocrity, and transform families, teams, and organisations—for the glory of God and the good of the world—building legacies that endure eternally.
