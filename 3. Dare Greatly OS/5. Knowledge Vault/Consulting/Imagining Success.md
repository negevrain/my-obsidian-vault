Imagining Success
	1.	What does success look like for your organization in 5 years?
	•	Picture the tangible outcomes and achievements. What specific goals have you reached?
	2.	What are the key milestones you hope to achieve along the way?
	•	Identify the significant steps or events that will mark progress towards your vision.
	3.	How will the lives of those you serve be different because of your work?
	•	Think about the positive changes and impacts on the individuals and communities you serve.
	4.	What new services or programs do you envision offering?
	•	Consider any innovative or expanded offerings that will enhance your mission.
	5.	How do you see your team evolving to meet future challenges?
	•	Reflect on the growth and development of your staff and volunteers.
	•	What existing jobs will be filled? By whom? Describe the people on your team.
	•	What new jobs will be created?
	6.	What partnerships or collaborations would you like to establish?
	•	Visualise who you’re allied with to achieve the mission.
	•	Identify potential allies and partners who can help amplify your impact.
	7.	How will your organization’s impact on the community change or grow?
	•	Envision the broader effects of your work on the community as a whole.
	8.	What new communities or groups would you like to reach?
	•	Think about expanding your reach to new demographics or areas.
	9.	How will you measure the success of your community impact?
	•	Define the metrics or indicators that will help you track and evaluate your success.
Reflecting on these questions can help you create a vivid and inspiring vision for the future of your organization. What stands out to you as you think about these questions?
