Quotes that have shaped my leadership

Sometimes it is better to ask for forgiveness than to seek permission.

I don’t know who coined this popular idiom, but it is something that has shaped my decision-making, and I have my father to thank for it. It’s meaning can be taken several ways, including simply using it as an excuse for doing whatever you want to do (taken a very self-centred approach to life). My dad, however, showed me that it has more to do with values and principles. In other words, there will be times as a leader when you must do what it right (strategically, ethically, morally and spiritually), even when you know that the decision is likely to upset people around you.
