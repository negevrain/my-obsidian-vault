Setting up a new school
The incorporated governing body of a proposed new school is required to submit an application for accreditation to the Board.
Each application is assessed according to a number of criteria, such as the proposed school's:
	•	educational program and student welfare processes
	•	resources
	•	financial viability
	•	improvement processes
Government funding is available for eligible applicants. Applications for funding eligibility must also be made to the Board.
