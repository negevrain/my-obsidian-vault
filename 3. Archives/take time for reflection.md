A successful approach to sustained high performance, we have found, must pull together all of these elements and consider the person as a whole

Making such a connection, we have found, requires regularly stepping off the endless  
treadmill of deadlines and obligations to take time for reflection.  
On the playing field or in the boardroom, high performance depends as much on how people renew and recover energy as on how they expend it, on how they manage their lives as much as on how they manage their work. When people feel strong and re- silient-physically, mentally, emotionally, and spiritually- they perform better, with more passion, for longer. They win, their families win, and the corporations that employ them win.
