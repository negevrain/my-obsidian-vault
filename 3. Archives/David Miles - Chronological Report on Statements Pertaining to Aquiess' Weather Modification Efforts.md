2008
	•	Dale Frankel:
	•	"At the end of June 06, our local radio announcer, Dave Lennon, interviewed David about his ability to influence rainfall. The season was becoming desperate and in that interview, David Miles stated that he had instigated an event that would take 30 days to come to fruition. On the 25th of July an "unusual" rainfall event came in over the north west of the country. Our weather bureau confidently forecast that this event would only spread over NSW in the east of Australia. David told me on a phone call that he was directing it over us in the Wimmera. We had 28mm of rain on the 28th of July and Dave Lennon from the ABC has been silent since."
	•	"In spring 07, after a meeting of Aquiess clients, a request for 50mm was put forward. In October and November, we received 55mm and there were reports of farmers having their best season ever."
	•	"How do you reconcile, when our multi-million dollar weather bureau tells us to expect 5-10mm as on the 15th of May this year and after a request to David for at least 25mm, we receive 35mm."
	•	"Our order to David was for 25mm in June, we have had 27mm."

	•	John Reichenbach:
	•	"Farming approx. 1600 ha in the northern Wimmera, 65 km north west of Horsham. On mixed soil types I mainly grow cereals (wheat, barley, oats, triticale) legumes (lentils, peas beans, chickpeas, lupins) and run sheep."
	•	"I first met David Miles at a VFF meeting in February 2006, where he was offering to supply the rainfall needs for a season as required. I was attracted to his approach and attitude, including his offer which included the statement “If I cannot deliver the agreed amount then it is of no cost to you”."
