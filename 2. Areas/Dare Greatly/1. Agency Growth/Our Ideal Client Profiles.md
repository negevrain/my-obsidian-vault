## **Brand Manifesto: Dare Greatly Solutions**

Dare Greatly Solutions is a transformation agency founded in 2015. We exist to grow organisations that aspire to be well-run and deeply impact their communities—especially those serving the poor, the disconnected, the lost, the voiceless, and the marginalised across Australia.

We coach Christian leaders who see their leadership as ministry, whether they’re pastors, politicians, or CEOs. We strengthen churches, not-for-profits, and purpose-driven enterprises that transform lives in Australia. And we build scalable coaching ecosystems that multiply influence, deepen engagement, and foster sustainable change.

Our work is rooted in integrity, excellence, impact, and connection. We believe transformation flows from the inside out—from the leader to the organisation to the community. That’s why we serve those who dare greatly: leaders and organisations committed to serving others with clarity, courage, and conviction.

## INDIVIDUAL CLIENTS

**Core (Inner Circle)**

- **Christian leaders who aspire to lead like Jesus**: Pastors, politicians, CEOs, business owners, and entrepreneurs motivated by their God-given calling to lead well, serve others, and transform lives.

- **Ministry Couples & Families**: Christian leaders, their spouses and households facing the pressures of ministry, needing ruthless accountability to keep marriages and families intact.

- **Not-for-Profit and Social Enterprise Leaders**: Christian leaders who are passionate about social impact and/or introducing people to Jesus, but need clarity, structure, and accountability to avoid burnout and maximise follow-through.

- **Advocacy & Justice Entrepreneurs**: Faith-driven innovators who embody the Gospel through ventures that confront injustice and restore dignity (e.g., anti-trafficking, support for single mums, or addressing homelessness).

  

**Adjacent (Middle Circle)**

- **High-EQ Professionals**: Individuals who are curious, self-reflective, and motivated to optimise their leadership style and relational intelligence.

- **Fundraisers & Marketers in Mission-Driven Work**: People who need our ability to distill complex ideas into concise, persuasive narratives, resulting in sharper messaging and stronger calls to action.

  

**Future (Outer Circle)**

- **Emerging Leaders**: Young Australian professionals (under 30) shaping their leadership identity around the example of Jesus, their God-given calling, and Biblical convictions.

---

### ORGANISATIONAL CLIENTS

**Core (Inner Circle)**

- **Christian Organisations Serving the Marginalised**: Churches, faith-based not-for-profits, and social enterprises focused on the poor, disconnected, lost, voiceless, and vulnerable in Australia, that are already well-run, but aspire for more capacity, higher performance, and greater impact.

  

**Adjacent (Middle Circle)**

- **Christian Organisations Serving Broader Communities**: Businesses, media outlets, other not-for-profits and social enterprises that aspire to operate at a higher level—building capacity, scaling programs, and better serving their communities and constituencies but aren't specifically focused on the poor, disconnected, lost, and voiceless.

## **Pro Bono Clients**

Pro bono work is mission-driven, not margin-driven—limited to 10–20% of our time. We serve one NFP organisation per quarter, limited to those aligned with our Jesus-centred call to the poor/voiceless.
