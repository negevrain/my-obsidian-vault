6:30 PM

I was drunk when she called me the first time. That much I do remember. I'm ashamed to admit that now. But if I'm honest, back then, the call just pissed me off.

I can't really remember much of that night, before the call, except that It was 10 o'clock when i first heard the klaxon alarm go off beside me. I was sitting in my chair in the corner of the lounge. I'd  drifted into a stupor and woke a start, my head pounding. A half empty botle of cheap whisky and a glass on the table beside me, an empty bottle on the floor a reminder that I'd been hard at it since sometime in the afternoon. And that damn phone blinking at me, screaming at me to pick it up.

It wasn't just that she called me. I'm used to the punk kids in the neighbourhood prank calling me. Although I'm beggered how they got my number. Anyway, I would usually just tell them off, spraying drunken slurred vitriol down the line, calling them all sorts of vile names, which would only set the punks off into fits of laughter as they hung up on me.

No, it wasn't just that she called me that night. It was why he called, and more than anything, that stupid name...

"What...?", as angrily as my pounding head would allow.

"Is this Mike Decker?"

Not a punk kid this time. A woman, and somehow she knew my name.

"Yeah. What do you want?"

"Just a moment of your time to talk about your day.

"What about my day?", as something started to rise up inside me.

"You were sacked for being drunk on the job. And it wasn't the first time."

A statement, not a question. I had been sacked that afternoon. Phil, the site manager, had called me into his office a little after lunch and told me that I was being let go. Too many complaints from other workers apparently. But how could  this stranger know that?

"How do you know that? Who put you up to this? Who are you?"

I shotgunned the question at the disconnected voice on the phone, the stupor starting to wear off, replaced with a dull anger.

"No one put me up to this. I go by many names. You may call me Christmas, and I've been watching you for a long time."

And that was when dull anger exploded into rage.

"Go away. Leave me the hell alone."

And before "Christmas" could say another word, i killed the call, and slammed the phone down on the table beside me.

In the sudden silence and prevailing gloom of my lounge. I shuddered before sitting motionless for I don't know how long, trying to compose myself, trying to force my sodden brain to figure out what just happened.

At some point, I know that I reached for the bottle of whiskey and poured another drink. But for the time in a very long time, i couldn't put the glass to my lips. The phone call had rocked me.

It was more an unnecessary interruption to my drinking. The call from Christmas forced me to remember. And that's what really pissed me off.

I didn't want to remember... anything. I wanted to forget everything.

I needed that drink. But why couldn't i take even the littlest sip.

And the phone rang again.

The clock on wall above the tv read 11.58. How i could i lose almost 2 hours?

"What...?", a little less drunk this time. And barely keeping my anger in check.

"Mike, it's me again. Christmas."

I wanted to hang up on her so badly.  But...
