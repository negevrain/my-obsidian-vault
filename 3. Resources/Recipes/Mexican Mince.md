Mexican Mince

Ingredients:
	•	500g ground beef
	•	1 medium onion, finely chopped
	•	2 cloves garlic, minced
	•	1 bell pepper, diced
	•	1 tsp chili powder
	•	1 tsp ground cumin
	•	1/2 tsp paprika
	•	1/4 tsp dried oregano
	•	1/4 tsp garlic powder
	•	Salt and pepper to taste
	•	2 tbsp tomato paste
	•	1/2 cup beef broth or water
	•	Fresh cilantro, chopped (for garnish)
	•	Lime wedges (for serving)
Instructions:
	1.	Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the ground beef and onion, cooking until the beef is browned and the onion is soft.
	2.	Add the minced garlic and diced bell pepper, cooking for another 2-3 minutes until fragrant.
	3.	Stir in the chili powder, cumin, paprika, oregano, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Cook for about 1 minute to release the flavors.
	4.	Mix in the tomato paste and beef broth. Reduce the heat to low and simmer the mixture for 10-15 minutes, or until most of the liquid has evaporated and the mixture is thick.
	5.	Taste and adjust the seasoning if necessary. Remove from heat.
	6.	Serve the mince in warm taco shells or soft tortillas, garnished with fresh cilantro and a squeeze of lime juice.
Enjoy your Mexican mince tacos with your favorite toppings like shredded cheese, sour cream, diced tomatoes, and avocado!
