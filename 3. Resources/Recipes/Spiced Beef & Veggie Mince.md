## Ingredients 
•  1 kg (2.2 lbs) lean beef mince (90% lean for high protein, low fat)
•  1 small onion, finely diced (optional, ~5g carbs total; omit for strict no-carb)
•  2 cloves garlic, minced
•  1 medium zucchini, grated (low-carb veggie, ~2g net carbs per serving)
•  100g (3.5 oz) mushrooms, finely chopped (umami boost, ~1g net carbs)
•  1 cup spinach, chopped (negligible carbs, nutrient-dense)
•  1 tbsp olive oil (or avocado oil for cooking)
•  1 tbsp tomato paste (concentrated flavour, ~2g carbs total)
•  1 tsp smoked paprika (for smoky depth)
•  1 tsp ground cumin (warm, earthy kick)
•  1/2 tsp chili powder (adjust for spice preference)
•  1 tsp dried oregano (herbaceous balance)
•  1/2 cup beef bone broth (collagen-rich, no carbs)
•  Salt and black pepper to taste
•  Fresh parsley, chopped (for garnish, optional)
•  1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce (optional, ~1g carbs; check for sugar-free)


## Instructions
1.  Heat the pan: Warm olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. If using onion, sauté for 2-3 minutes until soft and fragrant. Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds. (Skip onion for no-carb.)
2.  Brown the mince: Add beef mince, breaking it apart with a wooden spoon. Cook for 5-7 minutes until browned, seasoning with salt and pepper. Drain excess fat if using less lean mince.
3.  Add veggies: Stir in grated zucchini and chopped mushrooms. Cook for 3-4 minutes until softened, letting the veggies release their moisture and meld with the beef.
4.  Spice it up: Add smoked paprika, cumin, chili powder, and oregano. Stir for 1 minute to toast the spices and release their aroma.
5.  Build the flavour: Mix in tomato paste and Worcestershire sauce (if using). Pour in bone broth, stirring to combine. Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer for 10 minutes, allowing the flavours to deepen and the liquid to reduce slightly.
6.  Finish with greens: Stir in chopped spinach and cook for 1-2 minutes until wilted. Taste and adjust seasoning with salt, pepper, or extra chili for a bold kick.
7.  Serve: Garnish with fresh parsley for a vibrant touch. Serve hot as a standalone dish, over cauliflower rice (~2g carbs per cup), or wrapped in lettuce leaves for a no-carb option.
