I read the scripture about Jesus riding into Jerusalem. Asked God to show me where I was in the story. He showed me that I was at the back of the crowd, but I wanted so badly to be at the front. Too awkward, scared, embarrassed. God, what do you want to do with this…?

Words from God:

I remember a word that said something like … I will be on stage in front of a huge crowd.

Also, if I knew the scope and size of what God had planned for me, I would run away.

Also, word from Peter Patterson, about being behind the scenes for now, but the time would come where I would be in the limelight, at the front of the stage. 

Helen’s vision for me that I’m supposed to be a general leading an army, but I’m a small child???
