

A friend of mine preaches his last sermon today. From tomorrow, he’s no longer the pastor of a church. After years of faithful service, he walking away.

It’s the culmination of an incredibly difficult season for him and his family. DO I EXPLAIN A BIT OF WHAT HAPPENED?

As I listened, I wondered if his decision to leave would have been the same had he not endured so much heartache in the previous 6 months or so. I wondered what the will of God was in the midst of the circumstances.

I also wondered what the difference might have been had he had someone journeying with him and the church as they dealt with the issues. What it have made a difference in the end? Would he still be walking away from his role? Or would we still have my friend serving as a pastor.
