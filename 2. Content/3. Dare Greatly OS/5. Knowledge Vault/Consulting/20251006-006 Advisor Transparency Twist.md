**Content**:
- Advisors shine by spotting blind spots, forging key intros (investors, hires, clients), guiding hiring/firing, and foreseeing hurdles.
- Traits of solid ones: Small equity comp, clear expectations, hands-on grit, time respect.
- The twist: True help demands raw transparency—sharing personal discomforts and leadership "squishy edges," not just business data. Many issues stem from the leader's fears or ownership gaps.
- Pitfall: Investors often can't handle that depth; seek advisors open to your deepest vulnerabilities for fearless growth.
- For you as advisor: Invite clients to "go there" to amplify impact—builds trust and tools like a CEO's secret weapon.
