![[Garlic-Herb Chicken Rissoles 4.jpg]]## Ingredients:
500g chicken mince
1 large egg
30g almond flour
2 tbsp finely chopped fresh parsley (or 1 tsp dried)
1 tsp garlic powder 
1 tsp onion powder
½ tsp smoked paprika
½ tsp salt + cracked pepper
1 tbsp avocado oil (for cooking)

Optional: 1 tbsp grated parmesan or a squeeze of lemon zest post-cook


## Method:
1. In a bowl, combine chicken mince, egg, almond flour, herbs, spices. Get your hands in—pretend you’re sculpting tiny joy-mountains.

2. Form 8 flat patties (like mini UFOs). Press a thumbprint in the center so they cook evenly and look artsy.

3. Heat oil in a pan over medium. Cook 4-5 mins per side till golden and 75°C inside.

4. Let the rissoles rest 2 mins. Serve with a side of rocket + avocado half, drizzled in olive oil.
