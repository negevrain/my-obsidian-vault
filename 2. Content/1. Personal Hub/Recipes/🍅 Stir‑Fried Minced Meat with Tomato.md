## Ingredients

- **500 g minced beef or pork** (higher fat = better keto macros)
- **2 tablespoons avocado oil** (or olive oil / beef tallow)
- **3 cloves garlic**, minced
- **1 tablespoon fresh ginger**, grated
- **1 cup diced fresh tomatoes** (no added sugar)
- **2 tablespoons coconut aminos**
- **1 teaspoon fish sauce**
- **½ teaspoon smoked paprika** (optional, adds depth)
- **½ teaspoon sesame oil** (optional)
- **1 cup keto‑friendly vegetables**, such as:
    - zucchini
    - broccoli
    - mushrooms
    - spinach
    - capsicum (in moderation — slightly higher carbs but still workable)
- **Salt + pepper**, to taste
- **Fresh basil or green onions**, for garnish
- **Cauliflower rice**, for serving

---

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a pan over medium‑high heat.
2. Add **garlic and ginger**, sauté until fragrant (1–2 minutes).
3. Add the **minced meat**, breaking it up and browning well.
4. Once browned, stir in the **tomatoes or tomato paste**. Cook 2–3 minutes until they reduce and intensify.
5. Add the **coconut aminos**, **fish sauce**, paprika, and sesame oil. Let it bubble for 1–2 minutes.
6. Add your **low‑carb vegetables** and stir‑fry until crisp‑tender.
7. Taste and adjust seasoning — add salt, pepper, extra aminos, or a splash more tomato if desired.
8. Serve over **cauliflower rice** and garnish with basil or green onions.


Notes

A high‑protein, low‑carb (keto) stir fry with a bright, savoury tomato base. Best served with cauliflower rice. Add chilli flakes for heat. Works with beef or pork mince.
