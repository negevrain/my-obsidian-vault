Ingredients (for 14 pikelets):

•  3 large eggs

•  ⅓ cup heavy whipping cream

•  2 scoops (60g) unflavored whey protein isolate

•  1½ tbsp coconut flour

•  1½ tsp baking powder

•  ¼ tsp xanthan gum

•  1-2 tbsp keto sweetener (optional)

•  1 tsp vanilla extract (optional)

•  Butter or ghee for greasing

Steps:

1.  Whisk eggs and cream until foamy.

2.  Stir in protein powder, coconut flour, baking powder, xanthan gum, sweetener, and vanilla until smooth (use a mixer for fluff).

3.  Heat skillet on medium, grease lightly, drop 1½ tbsp batter per pikelet (they spread).

4.  Cook 1.5 min per side on low till golden—watch, they burn like forgotten promises.

Per six: 310 cal, 32g protein, 18g fat, 1.8g net carbs. Store in fridge or freeze.
