Team Building Process

	1.	Gather
	•	Jesus was a gatherer - people loved to be around him
	•	Jesus had a welcoming, gathering persona
	•	Smile, show interest, remember names

	2.	Motivate

	•	See where they are, but see who they can be, will be - speak to people’s potential. People become what the influential people around them say they can become.

	3.	Train

	•	Jesus spent three years teaching people HOW to become what He saw.
	•	Show people how... set people up for success

	4.	Mobilise
	•	Jesus sent people out


	⁃	The order is important.
	⁃	The process can be done in teams. The leader doesn’t have to be able to all steps.


LEADERSHIP DEVELOPMENT - selecting and developing leaders

Leadership Pathway

Matching people with their passion and gifting

Level 1 - Leading Self
	•	Life of integrity
	•	My relationship with God and other people

Level 2 - Leading Others
	•	Degree of influence over small group of people
	•	Hands on
	•	Pastoral care, planning 
	•	Values - growth of the people in the group

Level 3 - Leading Leaders
	•	You find joy in the success of other leaders
	•	Means giving up some tasks, letting others do them
	•	Giving up power and influence

Level 4 - Leading Leaders of Leaders
	•	More planning and long range thinking
	•	Focus on group more than the individual

Level 5 - Leading a Ministry(or Department)


Level 6 - Leading a Congregation


Level 7 - Leading a Movement
	•	Leading change
	•	Strategic architect


A. People just learning 
B. Doing it well
C. Doing it exceptionally well - master the level - possibly ready to move to next level

Goal should be to select and train as many leaders as possible.
Another goal is to increase number of volunteers in church.
