The game was close, and pretty much went down to the wire. But in the end the Brisbane Heat beat the Hobart Hurricanes by 12 runs in last night’s must-win BBL cricket match.

One of the brilliant features about the game was having the Brisbane Heat captain, Usman Khawaja, miked up, so we could hear what he was saying to his team during the game.

It was incredibly insightful, adding a different dimension to the game. And highlighted starkly that the final result was as much due to his leadership as it was the talents of his bowlers and batters.

In fact, there are several takeaways from the Uzzie’s leadership example that have applications and implications for leaders in other fields. Here’s the 5 that stuck out the most.

	1.	He was Calm - laughing, smiling … by comparison, the opposition captain was grumpy…
	2.	He had a plan, but was able to be flexible enough when the plan didn’t fit the circumstances.
	3.	He backed his team - trusted his team to do their respective jobs. Kept them accountable, 
	4.	He coached his team on the go to maximise their performance.
	5.	He was willing to give the least experienced player the opportunity to stand up and show what he was made of at the MOST crucial time in the match. It’s the last over of the game, and Spencer Johnson is handed the ball. X runs are needed by the opposition to win, and they have their MOST destructive player at the crease to face the final 6 deliveries. The young, very talent, but experienced fast bowler has NEVER been in a position like this.

Sport was much to offer, whether we into it or not. Good leaders do well when they stay eyes open to the lessons. Exceptional leaders are found in all arenas and fields of human activity.
