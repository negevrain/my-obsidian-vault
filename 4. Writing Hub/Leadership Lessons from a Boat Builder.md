
It's hard to imagine what went through Noah's head when God told him to build a boat.

I wonder where he was.

I wonder what he was doing at the time when he heard God say, "Noah build me a boat."

You wouldn’t be surprised if he questioned is own sanity to start with. Wondering if he was going mad hearing voices, and especially a voice saying build me a boat.

Do you wonder if he was in the middle of the desert at the time? Looking up and seeing blue sky overhead, barely a cloud, when this voice out of nowhere says, "build me a boat."

What sort of man does that? What sort of man obeys the voice of God when the command is so completely outrageously radical? What sort of man willingly chooses to look like a fool before literally the whole world?

The historical record in Genesis says that he was the only righteous person on the earth.
