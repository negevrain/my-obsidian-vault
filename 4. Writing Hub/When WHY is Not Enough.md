When WHY is Not Enough

I love Simon Sinek’s onion talk - the why behind what we do and how we do it. It is brilliant in its simplicity.

The principle has been around for a long time really. I remember when I was the GM of a christian radio station. I’d regularly remind the team that our purpose was to introduce as many people as possible to Jesus (our why). We just happened to do that using radio as the platform, and our “how” was to deliver the best quality on-air product possible, subject to resources and money and stuff, to ….

I’d tell my team that if it wasn’t for our “why”, we were just another radio station, and what’s the point of that.

But what if your why…?

I know a guy who seemingly runs down every rabbit hole of a potential “opportunity” for his business because, in his mind, it would be another expression of his why.

The problem is that I’ve seen him become so stretched once he emerges from each rabbit hole, that he neglects the 2-3 projects that are most aligned with his why, and are in fact the best expression of his why at this stage of his organisation’s development.

I kind of wish I could get a hold of him…
