Journey to find city/place called Heaven/Shammah... city based on description in Ezekial 40 to 48.

Main character : Ezekial

Man wandering - doesn't really know where he's going. Just has a compulsion to keep moving ... towards something / away from something.

His name is Ezekial. He's not attractive. Skinny. Grizzled. Sun burnt. Scars. Rat's nest for hair. Clothes worn and 1tattered.

The landscape he's wandering through is dystopian/desolate. Result of war, disease, natural disaster? Many years earlier? Like a desert - hot, unrelenting sunlight. 

Travellers clothes - pants, long sleeve shirt, boots, scarf and face mask, glasses/goggles, hat - like Calaban in Logan. 

He's left a society that is controlling, religious - based on left wing ideology - seemingly progressive. On the surface is flourishing but really is decaying. Run by a dictatorial regime - led by a smiling anti-christ. What is the leader's name? Gender? Leader is gay. Leadership is ambitious - eager to expand, take over other regions. Quick to shut down dissent - people suddenly and quietly disappear? Or public executions? Citizens are tattooed as children - or have a chip implanted. A poor representation of heaven? Why did Ezekial leave - his choice?

A series of episodes/encounters/moments ...

Encounters with agents of where he's come from. They're searching for a city that has only been a rumour until now. Also searching for people who have escaped - why? And why are they especially hunting down Ezekial?

Key moment - gets cared for by a couple who minister to his heart as much as his body. Who are they? How did he get there? Where in his story does this happen? Based on short story I wrote as a teenager.

Key moment - Ezekial is "pursued" by a man on the road. Thinks the man is an agent. What is the man's intent? For good or ill? Becomes a defining moment in Ezekial's journey. The man points Ezekial towards The City (description based on passage in Book of Ezekial). Helps Ezekial to discover that The City has been where his heart has been taking him all along.

Final Key Moment??? - stumbles across a group of people/kids. He leads them to The City. It becomes a flight, fleeing from the agents.

He didn't know where he was. He didn't even know how he got to where he was.

He just knew that one day - he couldn't say how many weeks earlier - he'd become aware that he was on the move. And from that moment, he followed the road. It might have been a highway in a previous life. It was certainly a road that was busy once upon a time, before… something happened.

His name is Ezekial. He might have been attractive before he began his journey. But not anymore, not after days and weeks (and month…?) of exposure. He’s gaunt, grizzled. His skin is leathery, like an old man who has spent too many days working in the sun without sunscreen. He has scars over his arms and back and one his left cheek as well about an inch long that starts near the corner of his mouth, a testament to some trial, but he has no recollection what that might have been. He fingers the scars on his arms through his shirt when he’s lost in thought, trying to remember the past. Rat's nest for hair. 

Clothes worn and a wee bit tattered. He’s wearing bone coloured work pants, the kind with too many packets, and a matching long sleeve shirt with pockets on the breast and sleeves. They’re all empty. he’s wearing black army surplus boots that are a size too small for him now. And his nose and mouth is covered my a make shift scarf, leaving only his eyes exposed. Hazel green eyes, full of sadness, anger, despair, although he can’t tell you why.

His backpack is light, just a steel torch with batteries that ran flat weeks ago, a half empty box of matches that he uses in case he fails to start a fire the old fashioned way, an empty water bottle that he refills from the occasional creek or stream that he comes across. He’s more careful now which streams he takes water from after drinking stagnant water and becoming violently sick for a day or more. Fortunately for Ezekiel, he had found a sheltered [something] that allowed him to recover with some degree of safety.

That was when the landscape was more friendly - [describe it…]

Now, the landscape has changed. he's wandering through is dystopian/desolate. Result of war, disease, natural disaster? Many years earlier? Like a desert - hot, unrelenting sunlight. 

The road stretches out before him, deserted, weeds growing through the cracks, now in a permanent state of disrepair. a black gash through red and brown desolation.

But at least it gives him the sense that he was heading somewhere... if only he knew where. So Ezekiel continues to wander - never knowing where he's going, just innately knowing that he must keep moving ... towards something, or maybe away from something.

It didn't take long for him to find a certain rhythm or routine. Most days, he'll start walking before sunrise, and keep going for as long as he could, until the oppressive heat simply becomes too much to bear. Where he walks now, shelter is scarce, although the rocky terrain and occasional tree along either side of the road would at least offer some respite, however briefly. By mid to late afternoon, he'd be walking again, trying to get as far along the road as possible before dark. Before the world around him became dangerous for lonely wanderers.

He'd do his best to find a suitable spot to spend the night. Most of the time, though, he'd be under the stars, possibly behind a builder or a tree, hidden as much as possible from the road that compels him during the day. Still, he is nonetheless exposed to the elements and the wildlife and any other dangers that may be out there with only a pitiful fire to offer any light, and very little comfort or security.

The further along the road he got, the more the issue of safety and security would weigh on him. Almost without thinking about it, he had started to walk beside the road, rather than on it, day by day keeping the distance between himself and the nearest rocks or trees or whatever as short as possible. He had no clue why, other than a growing sense of... something. That nothing would happen did little to dispel the sensation. If anything, he became more attuned to it the further along the road he got. 

He says out loud, “hunger is my only friend,” as though recalling a line from an old movie or something. Only silence responds. He must have run out of supplies long ago, if he even had any to begin with. Usually, he’ll snack on insects or small lizards if he’s quick enough to catch them and he’s discovered that the leaves of some of the bushes just off the road taste okay and provide a bit of a sugar hit.

today, though, he’s more hungry than any other day that he can recall. His stomach aches, and he feels faint.
