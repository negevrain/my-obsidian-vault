
So it's been about 2 months since I left the radio ministry that I helped to establish and then lead for over 20 years. For those who are interested, the station was 99.9 Live FM in Townsville. 




1. I am a person, not just a position. 

One of the most difficult things I dealt with since I left is the feeling of isolation. I've had remarkably little contact with people, especially those who I count as friends. Even those that I've reached out to have yet to return my calls or text messages.

Leaving has certainly helped me to sort out my true friends from those people with whom my relationship was ultimately just professional. And it turns out that I have less friends than I thought. 

Now before you start ragging on me, I'm not really having a pity party... at least not entirely. I'm simply saying that some of what passed as friendships while I was leading the ministry... well ultimately they weren't.

And those few people that God has brought into my life... that I do count as friends... mean more to me now than I ever imagined.

So, what's the lesson...? 

Well, I'm discovering is that you can be very close to the people you work with, and cultivate intimate professional relationships with others outside the ministry... but rarely do those relationships develop into true friendships. So when they do, when God establishes a heart connection between you and someone else that goes beyond the ministry, protect it and invest in it as much as you possibly can. My life would be that much more complete if I had learnt that lesson early.

Better still... cultivate relationships outside of the ministry with people who value you for who you are not what you do.
