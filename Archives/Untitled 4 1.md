#- Post-ministry isolation: Leaving Live FM brought a deep sense of isolation, with little contact from supposed friends, even after outreach.

- Friend filter: Departure revealed true friends versus professional ties, showing fewer genuine connections than assumed.

- Value of true friends: The few God-given friendships post-ministry hold more meaning than ever imagined.

- Lesson on friendships: Close work relationships rarely turn into true friendships; protect and invest in heart connections when they form.

- External relationships: Cultivate bonds outside ministry with people who value you for who you are, not your role.

- Identity beyond role: Realising I am a person, not just a position, shifts focus from title to self.

- Family priority: Family is the first call to minister to, above ministry work.

- Regrets as human: Leaving brought decisions I regret, but that’s okay—it’s part of the journey.

- Be still: Embracing stillness offers a path to peace post-leadership.

- Hope in transition: There’s a better yes coming, a promise to hold onto after leaving.
