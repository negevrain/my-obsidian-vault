Mentoring Program for Young Men

A 3-month curriculum for a mentoring program for young men can be designed to address the goals and needs of the mentees.

True North Philosophy - True North is your orienting point—your fixed point in a spinning world—that helps you stay on track as a leader. It is derived from your most deeply held beliefs, your values, and the principles you lead by. It is your internal compass, unique to you, that represents who you are at your deepest level.

```Plain
•   What is your dream? What are you doing or not doing to achieve that dream?What kind of man do you want to be? What are your values? What will you need to do to become that man? How does your actions align with your values.
•
•   The world may have very different expectations for you than you have for yourself.What are you afraid of?How does peer pressure affect you?What are you most proud of and least proud of?Have you ever been bullied or been the bully?
```

Possible Curriculum

Month 1:  
⁃ Week 1: Introduction to the program - issue values assessment  
⁃ Week 2: Who are you… really? Identifying your values, personal strengths and weaknesses (because we’re wired to have other people in our lives).  
- Week 3: Developing self-advocacy skills  
- Week 4: Building self-esteem and confidence

**Month 2: Social Skills**  
- Week 1: Introduction to social skills  
- Week 2: Communication skills  
- Week 3: Active listening skills  
- Week 4: Empathy and perspective-taking

**Month 3: Independent Living Skills**  
- Week 1: Introduction to independent living skills  
- Week 2: Cooking and nutrition  
- Week 3: Cleaning and organization  
- Week 4: Budgeting and financial management

Some resources that can help with developing such a program are:

- **Top Blokes Foundation**¹, which offers group mentoring programs for boys and young men over 3 to 6 months to increase their resilience, empathy, and respect for self and others in Australia.

- **Mentoring Men**⁶, which provides free long-term one-to-one life mentoring for men who are going through some challenges or changes in their life in Australia.

- **Apogee Strong**⁷, which is an online mentorship program birthed in 2020 based on the ideals our founders wanted to instill in their own sons. Apogee is focused on mentoring young men with a guiding philosophy designed to build character and help them learn to be providers and protectors.

- **Menslink**⁸, which aims to help young men build resilience, instil a sense of belonging, and enhance their positive potential in all aspects of life in Australia.
