### **Overview and Purpose**

This guide equips you to deliver the six-month Core Coaching Intensive with bold consistency and depth. You guide senior Christian leaders toward servant-hearted transformation that ripples from personal alignment through home, organisation, mid-program reinforcement, community disruption, and lasting legacy. Everything centers on leading like Jesus—disciplined, purposeful, for God’s glory.

Sessions occur monthly for 75 to 90 minutes, with unlimited Voxer or email support between. Track progress using 1–10 scales across four ripple areas at the start, midpoint (Session 4), and end. The guarantee stands firm: fully engaged clients experience at least a 3-point net increase in their average ripple score (from baseline to final audit), with no single ripple decreasing. If they do not, they receive a full refund plus unlimited bonus sessions at no cost until the 3-point net gain is achieved (capped at 12 additional months to protect your capacity).

Draw selectively from Tony Stoltzfus and Lead Like Jesus tools to evoke awareness. Prepare each session with prayer and pre-work review in HighLevel. This keeps delivery lean, automated, and mission-focused.

  

**Core Principles**

You coach as a sacred partnership with the Holy Spirit. Ask powerful questions that draw out God’s insights, listen deeply, and encourage client ownership rather than giving direct advice. Tie discussions to Scripture and pursue excellence as unto the Lord. Eliminate anything that does not serve inside-out transformation—this maintains focus and scalability.

  

**Session Flow (Applied to All Six Sessions)**

Follow this structure for every session to build momentum and consistency.

1. Relate (5–10 minutes): Open with prayer aligned to God’s purpose. Build connection and confirm the agenda.

1. Review (10 minutes): Celebrate progress and wins from previous actions or data.

1. Reflect (15–20 minutes): Explore challenges and insights using the questions bank.

1. Refocus (20–30 minutes): Co-create desired outcomes with the session’s tool.

1. Resource (10–15 minutes): Agree on action steps, prayer points, and homework.

1. Close (5 minutes): Affirm commitment, schedule ahead, and reinforce support.

  

**Session 1: Audit and Align Your Calling**

**Pre-Session Preparation** (sent one week prior via HighLevel)

- Intake form: Twelve questions for baseline ripple scores and anchoring Scriptures.

- Homework: Read Colossians 3:23–24 and Ephesians 4:1. Journal what leading “as unto the Lord” means today.

**Detailed Flow**

- Relate: Pray for clarity and courage in calling. Confirm agenda: audit, clarify calling, create Calling Canvas.

- Review: Note intake momentum.

- Reflect: Discuss challenges (e.g., “What fear caps your calling?”).

- Refocus: Build Calling Canvas (Why/How/What). Enhance with Lead Like Jesus EGO probe (Edging God Out vs. Exalting God Only).

- Resource: Set three to five SMART-ish actions, prayer points, homework.

- Close: Affirm their alignment step.

**Post-Session**

- Export notes within 24 hours. Update dashboard baselines.
