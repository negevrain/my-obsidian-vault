Training Program: “Kingdom Builders - Being With Jesus”

Workshop 1: Foundations of Kingdom Leadership

Objective: Establish the foundational principles of Kingdom leadership based on Jesus’ teachings.

Session 1: Welcome and Vision Casting  
⁃ Introduction to the program and its objectives.  
⁃ Casting the vision for Kingdom leadership.

Session 2: Core Values and Principles  
⁃ Identifying and aligning core values with Jesus’ teachings.  
⁃ The importance of integrity and character in leadership.

Activities:  
⁃ Group discussions on personal and organisational values.  
⁃ Vision statement workshop.

Workshop 2: The Disciples’ Journey and Leadership Development

Objective: Learn from the journey of Jesus’ disciples and apply these lessons to leadership development.

Session 1: The Disciples’ Journey - “Come and See”  
⁃ Exploring the initial call to discipleship.  
⁃ Applying the “Come and See” approach in leadership.

Session 2: The Disciples’ Journey - “Follow Me”  
⁃ The commitment to follow Jesus.  
⁃ Leading by example and building relationships.

Activities:  
⁃ Group discussions on discipleship stages.  
⁃ Relationship-building exercises.

Workshop 3: Identifying and Developing Kingdom Leaders

Objective: Understand how to identify and nurture potential leaders within a Kingdom context.

Session 1: Identifying Potential Leaders  
⁃ Qualities to look for in potential leaders.  
⁃ Jesus’ approach to selecting disciples.

Session 2: Developing Leaders  
⁃ Creating a development plan for potential leaders.  
⁃ Providing support and resources.

Activities:  
⁃ Leader identification workshop.  
⁃ Development plan creation.

Workshop 4: Training and Equipping Leaders

Objective: Equip leaders with essential skills and knowledge for Kingdom impact.

Session 1: Teaching and Training Methods  
⁃ Jesus’ methods of teaching and training.  
⁃ Applying these methods in modern leadership.

Session 2: Practical Skills Training  
⁃ Essential skills for effective leadership.  
⁃ Hands-on training sessions.

Activities:  
⁃ Teaching method analysis.  
⁃ Skills training exercises.

Workshop 5: Implementing Kingdom Leadership

Objective: Develop a plan to implement Kingdom leadership principles in your organization.

Session 1: Creating a Kingdom Leadership Plan  
⁃ Steps to create a comprehensive plan.  
⁃ Setting measurable goals.

Session 2: Overcoming Challenges  
⁃ Identifying potential challenges.  
⁃ Strategies for overcoming obstacles.

Activities:  
⁃ Leadership plan workshop.  
⁃ Problem-solving exercises.

Workshop 6: Sustaining Kingdom Leadership

Objective: Ensure ongoing growth and development in Kingdom leadership.

Session 1: Continuous Improvement  
⁃ The importance of continuous learning.  
⁃ Creating a culture of improvement.

Session 2: Evaluating Progress  
⁃ Methods for evaluating leadership development.  
⁃ Adjusting plans based on feedback.

Activities:  
⁃ Continuous improvement brainstorming.  
⁃ Progress evaluation workshop.

Day 7: Reflection and Celebration

Objective: Reflect on the journey and celebrate achievements.

Session 1: Reflecting on the Journey  
⁃ Reviewing key learnings and growth.  
⁃ Sharing personal experiences.

Session 2: Celebration and Next Steps  
⁃ Celebrating achievements.  
⁃ Planning for future growth.

Activities:  
⁃ Reflection exercises.  
⁃ Celebration event.
