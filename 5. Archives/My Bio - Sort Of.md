A LITTLE BIT OF INFORMATION ABOUT IVAN GREEN  
   
For the past 22 years, I’ve served as the CEO / General Manager of media ministries in Townsville (99.9 Live FM) and Toowoomba (92.9 Voice FM).  
   
It hasn’t always been easy. I’ve certainly learnt a lot, and continue to learn every day. But in the process, I believe that I’ve gained an array of skills, a wealth of knowledge, and hopefully a bit of wisdom borne out of experience.

As a result, the following are some of the areas that I am able to confidently provide advice, assistance and support:

- Leadership Coaching and mentoring the key leaders within the church or ministry. I’ve recently received my qualification as a CoachNet coach.

- Connection with the “unchurched”, because with connection comes an opportunity for influence. And with influence comes an opportunity for transformation.

- Strategic Planning, including execution, organisational alignment, and evaluation.

- Fundraising Ecosystems, including development of fundraising tools, development of major donor relationships, major gifts and capital campaigns, corporate sponsorship campaigns.

- Marketing, including creating an image and developing brand elements, using social media and legacy media (print, direct mail, radio and TV) and public relations activities.

Beyond all of that, I’m a husband to my beautiful wife, Janine, a dad to two amazing boys (Lincoln and Tyson), a son, a brother, a leader, and a friend. I’m a messy saint in a messy world. I’m a follower of Jesus Christ, and I’m called to serve Him!
