Deep and wide Andy Stanley

Our purpose is to reach unchurched people (people who have not attended a church for 5 years). To be a station that unchurched people find irresistible, and direct them to a church that unchurched people will find irresistible. We grade ourselves on how attractive we are to our target audience.

We leverage the consumer instincts of unchurched people.

We engage dual audiences (churched and unchurched) with the same message.

Our critics will be religious people.
