- I seek first the Kingdom of God. EXPLORATION: what does this mean for me and my business and my family…? How should this play out…?

But seek first the kingdom of God and his righteousness, and all these things will be added to you. — Matt 6:33 ESV

“Seek first” — an intentional and active pursuit-more than merely wanting something in passing. It conveys searching with priority and diligence. This single-minded pursuit of God forms the model for seeking first His kingdom, demonstrating that all pursuits-career, relationships, ambitions-integrate under the overarching aim of honouring God.

“the kingdom of God” — the dynamic realm where God’s will is done (cf. [Psalm 103:19](https://biblehub.com/psalms/103-19.htm)). At its core, the kingdom of God begins in the hearts of those who trust Christ and yield to His lordship ([Luke 17:20-21](https://biblehub.com/luke/17-20.htm)). It is both present—now, in this moment—and in the future.

“His righteousness” — means living a life aligned with God’s character and moral standards. It means:**Align My Values:** [Colossians 3:1-2](https://biblehub.com/colossians/3-1.htm)exhorts, “Therefore, since you have been raised with Christ, strive for the things above, where Christ is seated at the right hand of God. Set your minds on things above, not on earthly things.” This reorientation means continually bringing personal desires, relationships, and goals under God’s righteous rule.

**Depend on Divine Provision:** By seeking the kingdom as priority, I have the assurance that God is intimately involved in providing my daily needs. The command is to direct my energy and devotion toward God’s kingdom rather than be consumed with temporal concerns.

**Display Trust Through Obedience:** True seeking involves practical loyalty-repentance, prayer, obedience to God’s word, and a commitment to sharing the gospel. It is a holistic expression of faith rooted in God’s revealed truth.

It means seeking Him above all else, indicating trust in our God when it could easily be misplaced elsewhere.

Simply, God commands me to seek Him above all else, trusting Him when it could easily be misplaced elsewhere. Because God knows that nothing compares to Him — not cars, money, other gods, addictions, or people.

When times are tough, when they’re good, and especially when I’m tempted to worry or grow anxious, remember [Matthew 6:33](https://www.biblestudytools.com/matthew/6-33.html). Run to the Father. He’s trustworthy. Seek God above all else. His love is right. Seek first the kingdom of God and His righteousness, and **don’t forget the promise in the verse’s final portion: and all these things shall be added unto you. God is good**.

Seeking the kingdom—God’s sovereign, dynamic rule over hearts, communities, and ultimately, all creation—means aligning my life with God’s purposes—prioritising His values (justice, mercy, humility) over material concerns.

It means, before any business decisions, career move, relationship, or financial choice, the question becomes, “Does this (act/choice/behaviour/attitude/decision) honour the Lord and align with His reign?
