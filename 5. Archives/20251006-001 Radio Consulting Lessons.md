Lessons from 4TVR (Mareeba) Action Plan

Content:

- Sponsorship Backbone: Build rate cards, sales forms, and a pitch brochure (e.g., digital one-pager) to lock in sponsors. Target 6-10 businesses at $300/month as a baseline revenue model.

- Licence Playbook: Craft a solid business plan (peer collaboration key), negotiate transmitter loans (e.g., 2kW from local partners), and test frequencies onsite. Prioritise governance—audit constitutions early.

- Network Edge: Tap peer stations for templates (e.g., Live FM) and weave local connections for cross-platform growth ($880/year hint).
