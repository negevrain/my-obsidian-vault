1. Evangelism - We will cooperate with the Holy Spirit in sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ with as many people as possible. “He said to them, ‘Go into all the world and preach the good news to all creation.’” Mark

1. Family - We will nurture and defend the God-ordained institution of the family. We are called to defend and protect God’s marriage design and to minister in Christ’s name to those who suffer the consequences of its brokenness. We believe that children are a blessing from God; we are called to support and resource parents in raising, shaping and preparing their children for a life of service to His Kingdom and to humanity.

1. Individual Identity - We believe that every person is created by God in His image - “fearfully and wonderfully made” - intentionally male or female, each with unique character and personality, gifts and talents. We are called to help every listener see themselves the way God sees them, so that each listener lives life confidently and secure, the way God intended.

1. Social Responsibility - We believe that God has ordained the social institutions of family, church, and government for the benefit of mankind and as a reflection of His divine nature. Therefore, we are called to support these institutions, according to God’s design and purpose, and to protect them against destructive social influences. Such involvement is in obedience to Christ’s lordship over all creation and is required by His command to care for the well-being of all people.

1. Activism - While we fundamentally want to win every soul to Christ, we also know that we can’t stand back and ignore evils in society around us in the meantime. We will step out and take action to shape (change) our culture — and change the laws — for the sake of righteousness. We will campaign on behalf of the poor, the sick, the vulnerable, and the imprisoned. We are called to defend, protect, and value all human life.
