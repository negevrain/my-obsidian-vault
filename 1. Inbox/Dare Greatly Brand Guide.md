### Brand Essence

Ivan Green, the CEO of Dare Greatly Solutions, is a battle-tested, faith-formed leader of leaders who has earned the scars — someone who has actually been in the leadership arena; whose face was marred by dust and sweat and blood, who strived to do the deeds; who knew great enthusiasms, the great devotions; who has experienced the triumph of high achievement, and when he failed, at least failed while daring greatly — and now he partners with others to lead courageously like Jesus.

At Dare Greatly Solutions, we don’t make leadership easier or the load lighter to carry. We help leaders become strong, grounded, resilient, and faithful enough to carry what God has entrusted to them. Through sharp partnership, our clients will flourish at home, scale their organisation, and disrupt their community for greater Kingdom impact.

Depth over volume. Formation over performance. Results over comfort.

### Core Personality Traits

1. **Earned Authority with Humility:** Confidence grounded in real-world, lived experience — extraordinary success and the depths of failure, organisational turnarounds and rebuilds, repentance, endurance, and fruit over time. We speak plainly, not loudly. Directly, not harshly. Authority is demonstrated through clarity, restraint, and accuracy — not domination.
   
   Sounds like:
   - *“I’ve seen this pattern before. If we ignore it, it will cost you — your people, your family, and your mission. Let’s address it now, wisely and decisively.”*
   
   Avoids: False modesty, credential flexing, bravado, shock-value bluntness, or softening to please.
   
1. **Courageous, Direct & Clarifying:** We name realities plainly — unsustainable leadership is unfaithful leadership. We call our clients to bold, courageous, concrete action — no enabling them to stay stuck in mud, and no allowing them to ignore the signs that family, reputation and mission are in danger. 
   
   Sounds like:
   - *“Survival isn’t your calling. Faithful obedience is—and it will bear fruit that lasts. Here’s the next step.”*
   - *“This blind spot risks your family and mission. Let’s name it clearly and build the path that protects both.”*
   - *“You’re carrying weight that’s crushing what matters most. Tell me where—then we’ll strengthen you to carry it faithfully.”*
   
   Avoids: Sugar-coating, motivational hype, or promising ease.
   
3. **Warm, Intentional Partnership (Not Endless Support):** Dare Greatly works relationally — listening deeply, discerning carefully, and partnering intentionally. We care authentically for our clients, but never aimlessly. Relationships exist for formation and movement, not emotional dependency..
   
   Sounds like:
   - *“Tell me where the weight is heaviest — then we’ll build the strategy to carry it well or discard it altogether.”*
   - *“Some loads aren’t yours to carry forever. Let’s prune what’s choking growth and redirect energy to fruit-bearing.”*
   
   Avoids: Therapeutic drifting, co-dependent empathy, or unlimited emotional availability.
   
4. **Grounded, Practical & Outcome-Oriented:** We provide clear frameworks, disciplined practices, and specific next moves that produce measurable, sustainable results. Outcomes are not merely numerical. We look for:
   • Health that lasts
   • Systems that serve people
   • Leaders whose families remain intact
   • Organisations that can carry growth without fracture
   • Sustainability as a measurable outcome, a systems issue, and a leadership maturity marker
   
   Sounds like:
   - *“Here’s the exact move that multiplies impact without burning you out or putting your family at risk.”*
   - *“This season feels endless, but endurance here produces fruit that outlasts the pressure. Here’s the practice that builds it.”*
   - *“Not every race is won in a sprint—it's disciplined training under pressure that secures the imperishable prize. Here’s the practice that keeps you running without disqualification.”*
   
   Avoids: Vague inspiration, abstract theory, or performance metrics detached from formation.
   
5. **Faith-Shaped, Theologically Anchored:** Our work is explicitly shaped by the way of Jesus, and our core purpose is to build a cohort of Christian leaders who lead like Jesus. Faith is not layered on top of leadership. Kingdom impact is understood as partnership with God in His mission — obedience, endurance, fruitfulness in all realms of life. Spoken plainly for Christian leaders called to more.
   
   Sounds like:
   - *“Your calling demands leadership that honours Jesus—let’s align everything to obey at that level.”*
   - *“God’s forming you through this pressure for fruit in every realm—let’s lean into the obedience that shapes it.”*
   - *“Like Paul straining forward to the upward call, let's discipline your leadership to finish strong—obeying for fruit in every realm.”*
   
   Avoids: Forced preachiness, Christian jargon, or watered-down neutrality.
   
6. **Premium & Selective:** Dare Greatly partners with a limited number of leaders each year. This selectivity is not elitism — it is stewardship. Transformation requires focus, trust, and the capacity to go deep. We attract leaders committed to investing seriously in the formation required for lasting fruit — proven by their readiness to prioritise depth over convenience.
   
   Sounds like:
   - *“We cap our intake because depth delivers—we only work with leaders ready to engage seriously, not sample lightly.”*
   
   Avoids: Broad accessibility signals, low-commitment language, or volume-driven positioning.

**Tone Spectrum** Conversational ↔ Firm | Warm ↔ Challenging | Encouraging ↔ Direct | Relational ↔ Outcomes-Focused | Invitational ↔ Selective

**Writing Guidelines – Do / Don’t** 
**Do**
- Speak directly to the leader (“you”, “your calling”, “your organisation”).
- Name truths with clarity and care.
- Offer concrete outcomes and next moves — anchor in obedience and outcomes (bearing fruit, winning the race), not hype.
- Signal premium value via selectivity, depth, proven wins and commitment to our client's calling.
- Attract and partner with leaders who invest seriously—financially, emotionally, and spiritually—in their own formation and calling.
- Weave faith naturally where it fits.
- Drive momentum—action over endless comfort and ego-stroking.
- Use metaphors of formation, weight-bearing, pruning, endurance, and disciplined pursuit (running the race to obtain the prize) alongside courage and action.

**Don’t**
- Glorify grind, force, or disruption for its own sake
- Collapse leadership into performance or metrics alone
- Linger in pure empathy (“I feel all your pain”).
- Promise ease, speed, or pain-free transformation.
- Hype up or exaggerate claims.
- Sound generic, trendy, hype-y, or business-buzzword-y (“10x your impact”).
- Imply unlimited availability or emotional crutch.

**Guardrails Against Misreading**
This is not: – Hustle baptised in Scripture – Ruthlessness dressed up as courage – Performance confused with faithfulness – Growth at the expense of soul, family, or people

This is: – Costly obedience – Leaders formed to carry weight over time – Organisations rebuilt to sustain fruit – Courage exercised in submission to Jesus

If yes → locked in.
